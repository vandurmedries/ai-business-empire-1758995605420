# Company context for Cofounder

## Product

AI Founder Empire is a Roblox company-building game. Players run product, marketing, and sales cycles, improve departments, accept curated AI missions, and optionally buy Roblox-native utility upgrades.

## Architecture

- Roblox is the game runtime and official checkout surface.
- The external backend selects only from reviewed mission templates, ingests privacy-minimized events, exposes live configuration, and runs a constrained daily operator.
- Cofounder is the external business workspace for analysis, planning, reporting, and connected operational work.
- GitHub is the source-of-truth for code and change history.
- Roblox Creator Hub is the source-of-truth for booked Robux and payout reporting.

## Commercial principles

- Player value and retention precede monetization.
- Purchases are optional and must provide a clear, truthful benefit.
- No off-platform sale of Roblox benefits or currency.
- No guaranteed-income claims.
- The AI may automate analysis and allowlisted low-risk experiments, but prices, publishing, ad spend, public claims, and financial actions require human approval.
